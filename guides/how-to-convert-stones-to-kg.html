Stones to kg: how to convert your weight, with a quick reference table

Updated 2026-10-03

The numbers you need

One stone is 14 pounds, and one pound is 0.4536 kilograms, so one stone is 6.35 kilograms.

Going the other way, one kilogram is about 2.2 pounds, or 2.2046 if you want more accuracy.

Many people in the UK think of their weight in stones and pounds, while GP surgeries, pharmacies and most gym equipment show kilograms, which is why conversions come up so often.

How to convert stones and pounds to kg

First turn the whole weight into pounds: multiply the stones by 14, then add the pounds.

Then multiply that total by 0.4536 to get kilograms.

For example, 11 stone 4 pounds is 11 x 14 + 4 = 158 pounds, and 158 x 0.4536 = 71.7 kg.

Do not just multiply the stones by 6.35 and the pounds by 0.4536 separately unless you add both parts at the end, as forgetting the pounds is the most common mistake.

How to convert kg to stones and pounds

Multiply the kilograms by 2.2046 to get the weight in pounds.

Divide the pounds by 14: the whole number is the stones, and whatever is left over is the pounds.

For example, 80 kg x 2.2046 = 176.4 pounds, and 176.4 divided by 14 is 12 with 8.4 pounds left over, so 80 kg is 12 stone 8.4 pounds.

Calculators often show the answer as a decimal of a stone, such as 12.6 stone, and that 0.6 is a fraction of a stone, not 6 pounds.

Quick reference: 1 to 6 stone in kg

Half a stone (7 pounds) is 3.18 kg, and 1 stone is 6.35 kg.

2 stone is 12.70 kg, 3 stone is 19.05 kg and 4 stone is 25.40 kg.

5 stone is 31.75 kg and 6 stone is 38.10 kg.

These are handy for weight loss or gain goals, since a goal of 2 stone and a goal of about 12.7 kg are the same thing written two ways.

Getting readings you can compare

Weigh on the same scale, at the same time of day and in similar clothing, as two different scales can easily disagree by a pound or more.

Day to day readings move up and down with food, drink and water, so a weekly weigh-in usually gives a clearer picture than a daily one.

Pick one unit for your own records and convert anything else into it, rather than switching between kilograms and pounds week to week.

Tracking progress in stones

Half stone milestones (every 7 pounds, or about 3.2 kg) are a popular way to break a bigger goal into steps you can see.

A colour in chart with one box per pound, 14 boxes to a stone, makes a long goal easier to follow than a column of numbers.
