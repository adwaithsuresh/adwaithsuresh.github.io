Who I am

This policy covers Adwaith Suresh Pin Publisher, a private tool run by me, Adwaith Suresh, a sole trader based in England, United Kingdom. I sell digital products (printable PDFs and spreadsheet templates) through my Gumroad store. I am the data controller for any personal data described here.

Contact: adwaith007@gmail.com

What the app does

The app is a script that runs on my own private server. It creates Pins on my own Pinterest business account to promote my own products. Each Pin links to a product page on my Gumroad store. The app is used only by me, on my own account. It is not offered to other Pinterest users and cannot be connected to anyone else's account.

What data the app accesses

Through the Pinterest API, the app accesses only my own account:

  • Basic information about my own Pinterest account.
  • A list of my own boards, so it knows which board to post to.
  • The Pins it creates, including their titles, descriptions, images and links.

The app does not read, collect or store any data about other Pinterest users. It does not access other people's Pins, boards, profiles, followers or messages.

How data is stored and protected

The Pinterest access tokens and a simple log of Pins created are stored on my private server, which only I can access. Tokens are used only to make requests to Pinterest on behalf of my own account. They are never shared, sold or published.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through the Pinterest API with anyone.

Buyers of my products

If you buy one of my products, the purchase is handled by Gumroad, which processes your payment and delivers your download. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to that information. I receive the order details Gumroad provides to sellers, such as your name, email address and what you bought. I use these only to fulfil your order and deal with any support or refund request, on the lawful basis of performing our contract. I keep sales records for as long as UK tax rules require, normally six years, then delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you, and to ask me to correct or delete it. You can also object to how it is used. To make a request, email me at the address above and I will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Retention

The log of Pins created is kept for as long as the app is in use. If I stop using the app, I revoke its access to my Pinterest account and delete the stored tokens and logs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
